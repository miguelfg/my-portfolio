# AI News Summary 2026-08-27

## GAFAM y grandes empresas de IA

### OpenAI expands ChatGPT for Teachers to more U.S. school districts
[OpenAI News](https://openai.com/index/bringing-chatgpt-for-teachers-to-more-us-school-districts/) published on 2026-08-26 that OpenAI is expanding ChatGPT for Teachers to 55 additional school systems across 20 states, bringing the program to over 100,000 more educators and staff. The update also introduces a 16-state National Data Privacy Agreement.

### OpenAI says learning with ChatGPT is becoming continuous
[OpenAI News](https://openai.com/index/learning-never-stops/) published on 2026-08-26 that people generate up to 70 million weekly conversations devoted to testing what they know, while U.S. classwork/homework prompts exceed 460 million messages per week during the school year and stay above 180 million in summer.

[Simon Willison’s Weblog](https://simonwillison.net/) on 2026-08-26 highlighted [Qwen3.8-Flash-Next](https://qwen.ai/blog?id=qwen3.8-flash-next) as a multimodal MoE model and early preview of the architecture used in Qwen4; Willison also quoted Paul Dix on AI writing/refining 1M LOC into reliable software.

### OpenRouter pushes model choice into the editor
[OpenRouter Announcements](https://openrouter.ai/blog/tutorials/choose-best-ai-model/) published on 2026-08-25 a workflow for choosing the best AI model live in the editor.

## IA local y serving

### Ollama connects Claude Desktop to local and cloud models
[Ollama Blog](https://ollama.com/blog/claude-desktop) published on 2026-08-25 that Claude Desktop can be configured to work with Ollama as a third-party gateway provider, letting users switch between local models and cloud models.

### llama.cpp 0.3.0 adds multimodal support and serving fixes
The GitHub API shows [ggml-org/llama.cpp](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/releases) `v0.3.0`, published 2026-08-25, with dots3-note multimodal support, MTP for GLM-4.5-Air, tensor-split support, multi-sequence rollback fixes, and web UI improvements.
