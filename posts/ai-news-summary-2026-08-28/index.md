## GAFAM and Major AI Companies
- **OpenAI Expands Commercial Operations in Brazil**. OpenAI launched commercial operations in Brazil and is expanding its commitment to the country from São Paulo, with a focus on businesses, developers, research, and public institutions. The company notes that Brazil is one of ChatGPT’s three largest markets by weekly active users and that approximately 215 million messages are sent there each day. [Primary Source](https://openai.com/index/expanding-our-presence-in-brazil/)
