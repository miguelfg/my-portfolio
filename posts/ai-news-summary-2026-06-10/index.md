# AI News — June 10, 2026

## Chatbots and Agents

**Nemotron 3 Ultra Gains Prominence with Its Arrival on Agent Platforms**

- Ollama announced the availability of NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Ultra for long-form agent workflows, with 1M context and integrations such as Claude Code, Hermes Agent, and OpenClaw. vLLM announced day-0 support for the same model. Sources: [Ollama](https://ollama.com/blog/nemotron-3-ultra) and [vLLM](https://vllm.ai/blog/2026-06-04-nemotron-3-ultra-vllm).

**Claude Fable/Mythos is also a story about agents**

## On-premises AI and serving

**vLLM integrates serving into post-training with vime**

- vLLM announced vime as a post-training RL framework that uses rollouts served by vLLM alongside training components such as slime and Megatron. Source: [vLLM](https://vllm.ai/blog/2026-06-09-announcing-vime).

**Ollama 0.30 enhances the local inference foundation**

- Ollama 0.30 adds GGUF compatibility improvements via llama.cpp, enables Vulkan by default, and reports up to 20% higher throughput on NVIDIA hardware based on its benchmark. Source: [Ollama](https://ollama.com/blog/improved-performance-and-model-support-with-gguf).

**Nemotron 3 Ultra Strengthens the Bridge Between Agents and Local Execution**

- Ollama and vLLM announced availability or support for NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Ultra on June 4. Sources: [Ollama](https://ollama.com/blog/nemotron-3-ultra) and [vLLM](https://vllm.ai/blog/2026-06-04-nemotron-3-ultra-vllm).
