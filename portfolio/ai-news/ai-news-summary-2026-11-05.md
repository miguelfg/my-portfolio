# AI News Summary — 2026-11-05

The day combines announced advances in GPU infrastructure with moves in development tools and visual generation. The items below come from official publications or the authors themselves.

## 1. Major AI companies and GAFAM
NVIDIA announced general availability for CUDA 13.5 and GA support for Vera Rubin, alongside changes to CUDA Tile, MPS, and CUDA Python. [NVIDIA Technical Blog](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/nvidia-cuda-13-5-brings-nvidia-vera-rubin-ga-support/).

AWS explained how to use decision models with Amazon Bedrock AgentCore Evaluations, its platform for evaluating agents. [AWS Machine Learning Blog](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/use-decision-models-with-amazon-bedrock-agentcore-evaluations/).

## 2. Influencers and technical blogs
A Dharma AI post on Hugging Face introduces Dharma Field Extractor, a 2B-parameter model for extracting structured fields from documents. The authors also publish performance comparisons with frontier models. [Hugging Face Blog](https://huggingface.co/blog/Dharma-AI/dharma-field-extractor).

## 3. Generative imagery
OpenAI announced Images 2.6 with faster generation, GIF creation, more precise editing, and new API controls across ChatGPT, Codex, and the API, according to the company’s description. [OpenAI News](https://openai.com/news/?display=list) (entry “Introducing Images 2.6”).

## 4. Chatbots and agents
OpenAI announced its acquisition of Pierre Computer Company and said it will expand Codex tools for building, understanding, and reviewing code. [OpenAI News](https://openai.com/news/?display=list) (entry “OpenAI to acquire Pierre Computer Company, Inc.”).

The official OpenClaw repository records the stable v2026.11.1 tag, published on November 5. [OpenClaw v2026.11.1 release](https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/releases/tag/v2026.11.1).

## 5. Local AI and serving
No notable stable local-AI or serving release was confirmed in this capture. Recent Ollama and Unsloth signals corresponded to prerelease or beta versions. [Ollama releases](https://github.com/ollama/ollama/releases), [Unsloth releases](https://github.com/unslothai/unsloth/releases).
