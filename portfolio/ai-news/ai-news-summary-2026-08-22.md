# AI News Summary August 22, 2026

## GAFAM and Major AI Companies
- OpenAI launched [AI Futures](https://openai.com/index/introducing-ai-futures/), the blog of its new Strategic Futures team, focused on how to preserve rights and agency while integrating transformative AI.

## Influencers and Tech Blogs
- Simon Willison published a new post on how LLM stopped working following a change to the OpenAI Python library that removed `httpx`, as well as new reasoning trace capabilities and tools in the OpenRouter plugin for LLM. [Source](https://simonwillison.net/)

## Generative Imaging
- Microsoft announced [MAI-Image-2.6 launches at No. 2 on Arena ahead of Google, Meta, and xAI](https://microsoft.ai/news/mai-image-2-6-launches-at-no-2-on-arena-ahead-of-google-meta-and-xai/). The post indicates that the model ranks second on Arena’s text-to-image leaderboard and third in image editing, with significant improvements in text rendering.

## Chatbots and Agents
- Microsoft released [MAI-Code-1.1-Flash: Better, faster, at a quarter of the cost](https://microsoft.ai/news/mai-code-1-1-flash-br-better-faster-at-a-quarter-of-the-cost/). The model is already integrated into GitHub Copilot and VS Code, and Microsoft positions it as a more efficient and cost-effective option for coding tasks and agent-based assistance.

## On-Premises AI and Serving
- vLLM published [IsoExec: Unified Execution to Eliminate Trainer-Inference Mismatch in SkyRL](https://vllm.ai/blog/2026-08-21-isoexec). The paper proposes a unified execution contract to reduce the mismatch between training and inference in RL and reports results measured on an 8×H100 configuration.
